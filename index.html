About

Meet Your Leader

B Kishore Reddy

Congress Leader | Social Activist | Founder - BKR Foundation

Quthbullapur, Telangana
B.Tech, MBA
12th November

B Kishore Reddy was born and brought up in a renowned political family in Quthbullapur. His early exposure to political activism ignited a lifelong dedication to public service and development.

He has been deeply involved in politics, particularly within the Congress party, since childhood. Hailing from the Quthbullapur constituency, he has always been passionate about serving his constituency and advocating for their rights and needs.

Mission

To serve the people of Quthbullapur with integrity and bring sustainable development through inclusive governance.

Vision

A prosperous Quthbullapur with world-class infrastructure, quality healthcare, education for all, and empowered youth.

Journey

Political Journey

2006

MPTC Elections

Actively participated in MPTC elections from Congress Party, marking the beginning of his electoral journey.

2013

ZPTC Elections

Participated in ZPTC elections, taking responsibility of the cadre in his constituency for the Congress Party.

2014-2018

Assembly Elections

Played a key role in Assembly Elections for Congress Party Youth President.

2019

Malkajgiri MP Election

Played a crucial role in Sri A. Revanth Reddy's election as Malkajgiri MP in his constituency.

2020

COVID-19 Relief

Helped needy people during COVID through food distribution and hospital support. Accomplished tasks for Cantonment Hospital.

2022

Bharat Jodo Yatra

Played a key role in Jodo Yatra, ensuring basic needs and water supplies for all participants.

2023

Assembly Elections Support

Actively participated and played a key role in 2023 Assembly Elections for Mr. Kolan Hanumanth Reddy.

Present

Youth President

Serving as Congress Party Youth President, dedicated to empowering the youth and community development.
