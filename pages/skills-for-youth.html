About the Program

B Kishore Reddy Foundation is committed to empowering youth with essential skills for their future success. Our Skills for Youth program is designed to provide comprehensive training in aptitude and reasoning, helping young minds prepare for competitive examinations and career opportunities.

Course Offerings

Aptitude Training

Comprehensive training covering quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, data interpretation, and problem-solving techniques.

Reasoning Skills

Develop analytical thinking, verbal and non-verbal reasoning, critical thinking, and decision-making abilities.

Competitive Exam Prep

Specialized coaching for various competitive examinations with mock tests and performance analysis.

Career Guidance

Professional guidance on career paths, interview skills, and job placement assistance.

Expert Faculty: Our program is led by experienced faculty members with 10+ years of expertise in competitive exam training and career counseling.

Detailed Course Information

Aptitude Training

  • Quantitative Aptitude: Numbers, Percentages, Profit & Loss, Time & Work, Speed & Distance
  • Data Interpretation: Tables, Charts, Graphs, Case Studies
  • Problem Solving: Logical Puzzles, Pattern Recognition, Analytical Skills

Reasoning Skills

  • Verbal Reasoning: Analogies, Classification, Series, Coding-Decoding
  • Non-Verbal Reasoning: Figure Series, Pattern Completion, Mirror Images
  • Critical Thinking: Statements & Assumptions, Conclusions, Course of Action

Faculty Expertise

Our faculty members bring over 10 years of experience in training students for:

  • Banking & SSC Examinations
  • UPSC & State PSC Tests
  • Campus Placement Drives
  • Management Entrance Exams
